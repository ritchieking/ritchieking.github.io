# Awards and honors, 2012–2018

A record of awards for my visual journalism at Quartz (Aug 2012–Feb 2014) and FiveThirtyEight (Feb 2014–Sep 2018), compiled from public records on 2026-10-27. Each entry links to the record it was taken from. Entries are split into work I'm credited on by name and team awards that covered work I built or edited.

Malofiej editions are numbered, not dated: Malofiej N is judged in March of a given year and honors the previous year's work. Online categories carry a code whose last letter is audience size (A = under 5 million unique users, B = over 5 million).

## Other team awards for work I built or edited

- **Global Editors Network Data Journalism Awards 2016**: FiveThirtyEight named Data Journalism Website of the Year (June 16, 2016, Vienna). The submission bundle included the site's 2015–16 interactives. Also News Data App of the Year, large newsroom, for Swing the Election by Aaron Bycoffe and David Wasserman. Sources: [FiveThirtyEight, "We Won Some Prizes"](https://fivethirtyeight.com/features/we-won-some-prizes/) (June 16, 2016), [Knight Foundation](https://knightfoundation.org/articles/winners-2016-data-journalism-awards-show-growing-quality-promise-field).
- **Global Editors Network Data Journalism Awards 2018**: The Atlas of Redistricting named News Data App of the Year (May 31, 2018, Lisbon). I edited the project. Sources: [Data Journalism Awards on Medium](https://medium.com/data-journalism-awards/this-is-what-the-best-of-data-journalism-looks-like-6f1713d60479), [IJNet](https://ijnet.org/en/story/2018-data-journalism-award-winners-announced-gen-summit).
- **SND Best of Digital Design, 2016 competition**: Awards of Excellence to FiveThirtyEight for Gun Deaths in America (Features), 2016 Election Interactives (Special Events: U.S. Elections), and Staff Portfolio (Organizational Portfolio). Source: [SND 2016 digital results](https://snd.org/2016-results/).
- **SND Best of Digital Design, 2018 competition (40th edition)**: Bronze medals to FiveThirtyEight for The Atlas of Redistricting (Format: Use of Maps), Do You Know Where America Stands on Guns? (Format: Use of Data), the Olympics skiing piece (Sports), and the Election and World Cup lines of coverage. Source: [SND complete 2018 results](https://snd.org/best-of-digital-bronze-medal-winners/).
- **National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine 2017 Communication Award, Online**: Gun Deaths in America (per [Wikipedia's FiveThirtyEight article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FiveThirtyEight); not verified against the Academies' own list).
