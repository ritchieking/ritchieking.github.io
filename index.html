Ritchie S. King

I make charts. I build web applications for exploring them. I write narrative explanations that rely on them.

Currently a staff data visualization engineer at Netflix; before that, eight years in journalism as senior editor for data visualization at FiveThirtyEight, a visual journalist at Quartz, and a science writer and graphics editor at the New York Times. I also wrote a programming textbook called Visual Storytelling with D3.

Hack Your Way to Scientific Glory

FiveThirtyEight · Aug 2015

A hands-on demonstration of “p-hacking,” the process by which research scientists make small tweaks to their studies to yield a statistically significant, and thus publishable, result. My colleagues had the idea; I found a suitable research question and backing data sets and designed and built the interactive.
