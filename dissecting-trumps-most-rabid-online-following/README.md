# Dissecting Trump's Most Rabid Online Following (FiveThirtyEight) — archival copy

A self-hosted, archival copy of the FiveThirtyEight article "Dissecting
Trump's Most Rabid Online Following" (March 2017), originally published at
`fivethirtyeight.com/features/dissecting-trumps-most-rabid-online-following/`.
FiveThirtyEight was shut down by ABC News in 2025 and its archives taken
offline; this copy was reconstructed from the Internet Archive's Wayback
Machine (March 2017 capture) so the work remains readable.

I edited this piece at FiveThirtyEight, working closely with the writer,
Trevor Martin, and built its graphics; this copy is hosted as part of my
portfolio. All content © ABC News/FiveThirtyEight. Not affiliated with or
endorsed by ABC News or FiveThirtyEight.

Changes from the original: analytics/ads/tracking scripts removed, asset URLs
made local, links to other FiveThirtyEight pages pointed at the Internet
Archive, and an attribution banner added. The article and graphics are intact.
