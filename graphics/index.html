Static graphics

Between 2012 and 2018 I made hundreds of charts, maps, and diagrams for Quartz and FiveThirtyEight. Both sites have since been gutted, and most of the stories are gone.

This is the pile I managed to dig back out of the Internet Archive: a few hundred graphics from stories with my byline. Click any card to see it larger and find the archived story it came from.
